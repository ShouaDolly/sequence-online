Quick Guide

Your playable board spaces glow automatically. Tap a glowing space to play.

How to play

Play a card, then click its matching board square. J♣ and J♦ are wild placement cards. J♠ and J♥ remove an opponent chip unless that chip is protected by a completed sequence. The four corner spaces are wild for everyone.

With 3–6 players, the first player to make a sequence of five wins.
